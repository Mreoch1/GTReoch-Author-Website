Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

1. Current Website Behavior

Cookies are small pieces of information stored by your browser. The G.T. Reoch website at gtreoch-author-website.vercel.app does not use browser analytics, advertising cookies or visitor profiles. Its own code does not set cookies or store visitor information in browser storage.

The website has no visitor accounts, forms, newsletter signup or checkout. You do not need to accept optional cookies to read the pages or use the built-in video and audio controls.

2. Former Consent Preference

An earlier version displayed a cookie banner and stored your choice in a cookie named gt-reoch-cookie-consent, with an expiry set to one year. That banner did not enable an analytics service. The website no longer creates or reads this preference.

If you visited an earlier version, the old preference cookie may remain in your browser until it expires or you delete it. It is no longer used. You can remove it through your browser's settings for this site's cookies or site data.

3. Hosting, Fonts and Media Requests

Vercel hosts the website. Loading pages or media files sends technical request information to the hosting service, which may process it for content delivery, operation and security. This does not require an analytics cookie. See Vercel's Privacy Notice.

The design loads Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google's font servers and sends technical request information. See Google's Privacy Policy. The trailers and audio samples are hosted with this website and use native browser players, without embedded third-party video or social-media players.

4. When You Follow External Links

Amazon purchase links open Amazon's website. Amazon may use its own cookies and handles purchases under its own policies. See Amazon's Privacy Notice. A website you visit through an external link controls its own cookies and privacy choices.

5. Your Choices and Questions

Your browser settings let you review, block or delete cookies and site data. Removing the former consent preference does not affect this website's pages or media controls. Browser controls and privacy choices on other services are managed separately.

For more about technical requests and email contact, read our Privacy Policy. Questions about this website can be sent to Gtreoch@yahoo.com. This page will be updated if the website's cookie behavior changes.
