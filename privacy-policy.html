Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

1. About This Website

This policy describes the current G.T. Reoch author website at gtreoch-author-website.vercel.app. The site presents books, author information, trailers and audio samples. It has no visitor accounts, contact forms, newsletter signup or checkout.

2. Browsing and Hosting

Vercel hosts the website and delivers its pages, images, videos and audio. Requests to the hosting service may include technical information such as your IP address, browser information, requested resource and request time. Vercel may process this information to deliver content, operate its service and protect against abuse. This is separate from browser analytics or advertising tracking. See Vercel's Privacy Notice for its practices.

The site does not run browser analytics or advertising trackers. Vercel Web Analytics is not enabled, and the site does not load a Speed Insights tracking script. Its own code does not set analytics or advertising cookies. See our Cookie Policy for details, including the former cookie-banner preference.

3. Fonts and Media

The page design loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser makes requests to Google's font servers, which receive technical request information, including your IP address and browser information. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Book trailers and audio samples are files hosted with this website and played using your browser's built-in media controls. They do not use embedded YouTube, Vimeo or social-media players. Loading or playing a file sends a request to the website's hosting service.

4. Email Contact

The email link opens your email application. If you choose to send a message to Gtreoch@yahoo.com, the recipient receives your email address, message and any information or attachments you include. The message is handled through email services, rather than a form on this website. Please include only the information needed for your inquiry.

5. Amazon and Other External Links

Book purchase links take you to Amazon. Amazon handles its own accounts, cookies, orders and payment information; this website has no checkout and does not receive your payment details through these links. Amazon's handling of information is described in its Privacy Notice. Other linked websites also have their own privacy practices.

6. Questions and Changes

For questions about this website or information you have sent by email, contact Gtreoch@yahoo.com. For information processed by an external service, its privacy notice explains the available contact methods and choices.

This page will be updated when the website's features or described practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.
